# Privacy Policy — PawLetter

**Last updated:** August 24, 2026

PawLetter ("the App") is a private diary app for couples. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

## Information We Collect

When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive:
- A unique user identifier (UID)
- Your name and/or email address, if you choose to share it with the sign-in provider (Apple's "Hide My Email" is fully supported)

When you use the App, we store:
- The invite code used to pair with your partner
- Content you create: diary letters, calendar events, and timestamps
- Your display name and, if set, an avatar image URL
- Read/unread status of letters between paired partners

## How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to operate the App's core features:
- Authenticating your account
- Pairing you with your partner via invite code
- Displaying your shared diary letters and calendar to you and your paired partner
- Displaying your display name and avatar to your paired partner

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Data Storage

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), operated by Google LLC. Access to your diary content is restricted to you and your paired partner only, enforced through Firebase Security Rules.

## Data Retention & Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will delete your user document, diary letters, calendar events, and pairing information within a reasonable timeframe.

## Children's Privacy

PawLetter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Third-Party Services

- Sign in with Apple
- Google Sign-In
