Getting started

How do I connect with my partner?

After signing in, you'll get your own invite code. Share it with your partner however you like — Copy or Share works from the pairing screen — and have them enter it on their own device. Once they join, you're paired for good.

Can I use Letterfly on my own, without a partner?

Letterfly is built around a pair — letters, the calendar, and the memories map only make sense once you're connected to someone. You'll need a partner to get past the pairing screen.

Can I change my partner later?

Not from within the app right now. If you need to re-pair with a different account, write to us at the address below and we'll help.

Letters

What's the difference between a text and a voice letter?

When you write a new letter, you can choose either mode. A text letter is written; a voice letter is recorded right in the app. Either can still include a photo, a mood, a gift sticker, and a location.

How do Time Capsule letters work?

When writing a letter, you can schedule it to unlock on a future date — either one you pick, or one tied to a shared event like an anniversary. Your partner will see that a letter is waiting, but won't be able to open it until the date arrives.

Can I edit or delete a letter after sending it?

Yes. Open the letter and use the menu in the top corner to edit or delete it. Deleting a letter also removes any photo or voice recording attached to it.

Notifications

I'm not getting notified when my partner writes

Check that notifications are allowed for Letterfly in your phone's Settings app. If they're already on and you're still not seeing them, try opening the app once so it can refresh — then write to us if it keeps happening.

Account & privacy

How do I delete my account and my data?

Write to us at the address below and we'll remove your account, your letters, your shared calendar, and your pairing within a reasonable time. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we store and why.

Who can see what I write?

Only you and the partner you're paired with. There's no public feed, no discovery, and no one else with access to your letters.
