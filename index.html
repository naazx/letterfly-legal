Letterfly

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 12, 2026

Letterfly is a private letter-writing diary for couples. This policy explains, plainly, what we collect when you use it, why, and how it's kept safe. If a term here is unclear, write to us — the address is at the end.

What we collect

When you sign in

  • A unique account identifier, created by Sign in with Apple or Google
  • Your name and email, only if you choose to share them with the sign-in provider — Apple's "Hide My Email" is fully supported

When you write

  • The letters you write: their text, or a voice recording if you send one instead
  • Photos you attach to a letter
  • A place you choose to tag a letter with, if you add one — this is only collected when you explicitly attach a location to a letter, never in the background
  • Any mood, gift sticker, or reaction you attach to a letter
  • A future unlock date, if you schedule a letter as a time capsule

About your pair

  • The invite code used to connect with your partner
  • Your display name, partner nickname, and avatar image, if you set one
  • Shared dates you add to your calendar together
  • Read status of the letters you exchange

To deliver notifications

  • A device push token, so we can notify you when your partner writes, a time capsule is about to unlock, or a shared date is coming up

How we use it

Everything above exists to run Letterfly's own features, and nothing else:

  • Signing you in and keeping your account secure
  • Connecting you with your partner through your invite code
  • Showing your shared letters, calendar, and memories map to you and your paired partner — and to no one else
  • Sending you a notification when something in the app needs your attention

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing. There are no ads in Letterfly.

Where it's stored

Letterfly runs on Google Firebase — Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Storage, and Firebase Cloud Messaging. Access to your letters, photos, and shared calendar is restricted to you and your paired partner alone, enforced by Firebase Security Rules at the database level — not just hidden in the app's interface.

Keeping or deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and everything tied to it at any time — just write to the address below. We'll remove your user record, your letters, your shared calendar, and your pairing within a reasonable time.

Children's privacy

Letterfly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under that age.

Services we rely on

Letterfly is built on a small number of third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google)
  • Sign in with Apple
  • Google Sign-In

If this policy changes

If we update this policy, the change will appear on this page with a new "last updated" date above.

Questions about this policy, or want your data deleted? Write to us.
