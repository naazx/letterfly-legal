Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abuse

Letterfly has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior of any kind — this includes, but isn't limited to, threats, harassment, hate speech, sexually explicit content, and anything intended to harm, degrade, or endanger your partner or anyone else.

You agree not to use Letterfly to send content that is illegal, hateful, harassing, threatening, sexually explicit, or otherwise objectionable. Letters you write may be automatically screened for language that violates this policy, and may be blocked from sending as a result.

Reporting objectionable content

If you receive a letter that you believe violates these terms, you can report it directly from the letter using the Report option. Reporting sends us the letter and the reason for the report so we can review it.

Blocking an abusive partner

You can block your partner at any time from your profile. Blocking is immediate and:

  • Ends your pairing right away
  • Removes their letters, photos, and shared content from your view instantly
  • Prevents them from pairing with you again using the same account
  • Notifies us so we can review the account you blocked

How we respond

We review every report and every block. Where content violates these terms, we remove it; where an account's behavior violates these terms, we take action against that account — up to and including permanently ejecting the user from Letterfly. We aim to act on reports within 24 hours of receiving them.

Your content

You own what you write. We don't claim ownership over your letters, photos, or recordings — we store and transmit them solely so Letterfly can show them to you and your paired partner, as described in our Privacy Policy.

We may suspend or terminate your access to Letterfly, without notice, if you violate these terms — in particular the zero-tolerance policy above. You can also delete your own account at any time; see our Support page for how.
